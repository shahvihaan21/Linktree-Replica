ABOUT / PORTFOLIO

Vihaan Shah

I'm currently pursuing a BBA with a focus on Leadership, and much of my time goes into understanding how businesses actually work — not just the theory, but the mechanics of decision-making, strategy and organisational behaviour. I'm drawn to the kind of thinking that sits at the intersection of analysis and action, which is why Business Analytics has become a strong area of interest for me.

Beyond coursework, I spend a lot of time exploring things that genuinely hold my attention: investing, technology, psychology and the way these fields overlap in unexpected ways. I find that the most useful insights tend to come from connecting ideas across disciplines rather than staying within one lane. I read widely, take notes constantly and try to build a working understanding of things rather than settling for surface-level familiarity.

I like learning by doing. Whether it's writing about a concept to test whether I actually understand it, building small projects to see how tools work, or experimenting with ideas that might not go anywhere — the process of figuring things out matters to me more than having a polished result to show for it. That said, I do care about getting better at what I build over time.

My long-term direction is still forming, and I'm comfortable with that. I know I want to work at the intersection of business and technology, likely in a role that involves analytical thinking, strategy and some degree of creative problem-solving. I'm not in a rush to define a single career path — I'd rather build a wide base of skills and understanding now, and let the specifics come into focus as I gain more experience.

If you're here, you're probably looking for a sense of who I am beyond a resume. I'm someone who takes learning seriously, stays curious about how things work, and is building toward something meaningful — even if I can't describe the full picture yet.
